How Credits Work

Knowledge Is Your Currency

SkillSwap uses a credit-based system to make knowledge exchange fair and rewarding. Instead of paying money, members earn credits by teaching and use them to learn from other members.

Example of a teaching session

+20 Credits

A member earns credits after successfully completing a teaching session.

💎

Give Knowledge. Earn Credits.

Every completed exchange can contribute to your credit balance. You can then use your available credits to participate in learning sessions with other members.

The goal is simple: everyone gets the opportunity to teach, learn and grow.
